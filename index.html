The Off-Grid Ledger series

The Free Off-Grid Ledger Master Ledger

Off-grid living is mostly a numbers game. The homesteads that last are the ones where somebody wrote everything down: what the land cost, what the water system really ran, what the chickens ate, and what came back in.

This is a blank ledger you fill in yourself. It starts with the Year One topics from the first book, and a new section gets added each time another book in the series comes out. It's one ledger that grows with your homestead, so you never have to start over.

What's inside
  • Year One pages for startup costs, land, water, power, and shelter
  • Monthly entry pages for money in and money out
  • A year-end page to see what the homestead actually cost and earned
  • New year sections added as each book releases, at this same link

Print it, keep it on the kitchen shelf, and write in it. A paper ledger doesn't need a charge.

You'll get the ledger plus a short note when a new year's section is added. No spam, and you can unsubscribe anytime.
